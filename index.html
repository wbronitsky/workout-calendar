Add to your iPhone home screen

Firefox on iPhone currently ignores custom icons and shows a letter “W” instead (a known Firefox bug). To get the barbell icon, add the shortcut from Safari once:

  1. Open this page in Safari
  2. Tap Share (square with ↑)
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add

After that, use the home-screen icon like an app. You can keep browsing elsewhere in Firefox.
